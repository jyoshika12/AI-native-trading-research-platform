What does “work” mean here?

We will show how many trades gained money after costs and the average gain or loss per trade. We will also compare that average with ordinary holding periods of the same length in this invented sample.

Important limit

The prices and day numbers are invented. They do not represent real NIFTY or ETF history and cannot prove a real-world strategy works.
